Data Center

Fiber connectivity for data halls of every size.

XTND has strong experience providing fiber connectivity for data center applications. Pre-terminated MTP®/MPO trunks and harnesses cut installation time, while factory testing guarantees low-loss performance from day one.

  • MTP®-12 and MTP®-24 trunks from 24 to 288 fibers, 0.35 dB max
  • OM3, OM4 and OS2 harnesses with Type A / B polarity
  • High-density rack-mount patch panels
  • Glass-door, seismic and IP55 floor-standing cabinets

Ultra High Density

More ports, without the cable chaos.

Traditional high-density enclosures hit the port count but leave unmanageable clusters of cable at the back and components that are hard to reach. XTND's ultra-high-density approach keeps every fiber accessible, scalable and easy to maintain.

  • 48-port 2U panels and MTP®-24 trunks up to 192 fibers
  • Cable management, tidy and dump panels
