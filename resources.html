FAQ

Common questions.

Can you build assemblies to a custom length or fiber count?

Yes. Most patch cords, trunks, harnesses and breakout assemblies are built to order — specify length, fiber type, connector, polarity and jacket rating and we'll configure it.

Are your assemblies tested before shipping?

Factory-terminated assemblies are tested for insertion loss before dispatch. MTP® products are specified at 0.35 dB max.

Do you supply products for hazardous areas?

Yes. Our ATEX / Ex d rated wall-mount fiber patch panels are certified for Zone 1 and Zone 2 environments, supporting 12 to 144 fibers.

Which regions do you serve?

We have sales offices in Canada, the United Kingdom, the UAE and the USA, and ship to projects worldwide.
